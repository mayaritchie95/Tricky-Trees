Our specialty

Tree Removal

Tree removal is the heart of what we do. Whether it’s a towering pine leaning over your cottage, a dead ash by the driveway, or a stand of trees crowding your shoreline, our crew removes it safely and cleanly. We assess every job first, plan the drop or rigging, protect what’s around it, and leave your property tidy when we’re done.

  • Hazardous & dead tree removal
  • Storm-damaged & leaning trees
  • Tight-access & shoreline removals
  • Full cleanup and haul-away

Tree Pruning

Good pruning is about knowing exactly what to cut and what to leave. We prune for tree health, structure and safety—removing deadwood, reducing risk, and improving light and views without harming the tree.

  • Deadwood & hazard reduction
  • Crown thinning & shaping
  • Young-tree structural pruning
  • View & light improvement
