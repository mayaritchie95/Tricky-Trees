Our specialty

We specialize in crane-assisted tree removal

When a tree is too big, too damaged, or too close to a house, cottage or hydro line to climb and rig safely, a crane is the answer — and it’s where we shine.

Crane work lets us lift heavy limbs and whole tree sections straight up and away from what’s below, with no risk of dropping wood on a roof, deck or shoreline. It’s faster, safer, and far easier on your property than a conventional takedown — ideal for tight lots, steep terrain and hazardous trees.

  • Large, hazardous & storm-damaged trees
  • Removals over homes, cottages & structures
  • Tight-access lots, shorelines & steep terrain
  • Faster, safer, and easier on your property
